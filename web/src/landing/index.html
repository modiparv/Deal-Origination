Provenance, transitively

Click any figure and walk the chain: figure → inline-XBRL tag → source document (with its producing software and content hash) → filing date and registry transaction → the filing itself on the public register. Derived figures walk back through every input to its own source. A filed figure without a source document cannot be persisted.

Absence carries a cause

Most UK small companies file no public P&L. The engine never papers over a gap: every missing concept states its recorded reason — filed_without_concept, unparseable_format, parse_failed, not_reached, not_captured — as distinct, first-class states. A parse failure is reported as a system defect, never disguised as a data limitation.

Staleness shown, never hidden

Filed registry data is 9–21 months old by construction, and worse in the tail. Every figure shows its period end, filed date and age; profiles lead with the freshest machine-readable period. Reports state dated public evidence of absence — never the unevidenceable claim that a company is “not being marketed”.

What you will not find here: financial figures invented or computed by a language model, aggregator-sourced numbers, sample companies, or placeholder values. If a screen has no data, it says so. Derived metrics (EBITDA, margins, growth) and scores are Phase 2 and render as not_reached until the tested derive layer exists.
