A deal-origination screen built on the public register. Filed accounts become numbers you can filter and compare, and each one is a click away from the document it came from. Nothing is estimated, and nothing is computed by a language model.

Open the screen How it works

  1. 01

    Read the register, keep the provenance

    The engine enumerates a mandate's universe, ingests each company's filings, and parses inline-XBRL accounts into figures. A figure without its source document, tag, period end and filed date cannot be stored — the database refuses it.

  2. 02

    Absence carries a cause

    Most small UK companies file no public P&L. Where a number is missing, the screen says why — filed_without_concept, unparseable_format, parse_failed — and every count breaks three ways: matched, failed, could not be measured. The third number is never hidden.

  3. 03

    Staleness shown, never hidden

    Registry data is nine to twenty-one months old by construction. Every figure shows its period end and filed date; anything over two years is marked stale. The engine states dated public evidence — never that a company is “not being marketed”, which no register can prove.
